# New member FAQ

## How do I join the club?

Sign up on the Club Member Portal with your campus email. After login you can use the chatbot to ask about workshops, Builder Center, club events, and who to contact on the team.

## When are meetings?

General meetings are **Wednesdays at 6:00 PM** in the CS building, Room 101. Check the workshop index for special sessions.

## Who can attend workshops?

Any student with an AWS account (free tier is fine). Bring your laptop. First-time attendees should read the AWS account setup guide before the session.

## How do I get help?

Ask in the portal chat (logged-in members only). For account or AWS questions you cannot answer from the documents, direct members to the right campus contact below.
