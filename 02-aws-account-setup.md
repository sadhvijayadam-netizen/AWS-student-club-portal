# AWS account setup

## Creating an account

1. Go to [aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com) and choose **Create an AWS Account**.
2. Use a personal email you check often. Campus email works if your school allows it.
3. Add a payment method. The **AWS Free Tier** covers most learning workloads for 12 months.

## Free tier basics

- New accounts get free-tier eligible services for 12 months from sign-up.
- Always check the **Billing Dashboard** during hackathons so you know what is running.
- **Turn off or delete** resources you are not using after the event.

## Billing alerts

1. Open **Billing** → **Billing preferences**.
2. Enable **Receive Free Tier Usage Alerts** and **Receive Billing Alerts**.
3. Create a CloudWatch billing alarm if your instructor recommends it.

## Campus lab accounts

Some schools provide AWS Academy or Learner Lab accounts. If you have one, use the credentials your instructor gives you — do not mix personal and lab accounts in the same project.

## Security

- Never commit access keys to GitHub.
- Use IAM users or roles with least privilege — not the root account for daily work.
