# Publish on AWS Builder Center

## Why publish?

Builder Center is the public home for what Student Builders create. Hackathon teams **must** publish an article describing the Club Member Portal and how it could run on AWS.

## Steps

1. Sign in at [builder.aws.com](https://builder.aws.com).
2. Choose **Create** → **Article**.
3. Add a clear title, summary, and screenshots (sign up, login, forgot password, chat with sources).
4. Explain your architecture for AWS — Cognito, SES, S3, API, and AI — even if you demo locally.
5. Add tags: `#aws-student-builders-groups` `#buildonaws` `#amazon-bedrock` `#rag`.

## What judges look for

- Who the portal helps (new club members).
- What you built in the 70% phase (and 30% if applicable).
- Honest note about local demo vs live AWS (bonus if deployed).

## Tips

- Lead with screenshots, not long code blocks.
- Link to your repo if it is public.
- Publish before final judging when possible.
