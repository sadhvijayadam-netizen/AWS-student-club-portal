# About AWS Student Builder Groups

## Mission

AWS Student Builder Groups help students **learn by building** on AWS and share their work with the community through Builder Center.

## What members do

- Attend workshops and build projects.
- Publish articles on [builder.aws.com](https://builder.aws.com).
- Join campus hackathons like the Club Member Portal challenge.
- Connect with peers and AWS student programs.
